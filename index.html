About

From Labé to the screen

Born in Labé, Guinea, Mamadou Adama Diallo graduated in Sociology of Organizations at the University of Labé in 2019. He co-founded TABSAH Multiservice and worked as a customer service agent there from 2019 to 2022 — welcoming clients, keeping records organized, and resolving day-to-day questions with care.

During university he studied classical and contemporary sociological theory and took part in field studies using qualitative methods. That same attention to people and systems now shapes how he approaches web projects: clear structure, careful detail, and experiences that feel dependable.
